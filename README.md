# Dr. Munna Assist AI (ডা. মুন্না এসিস্ট এআই)

Dr. Munna Assist AI is a comprehensive Bengali & multilingual virtual healthcare assistant, interactive 3D/2D avatar doctor, anatomy visualizer, AI creative studio, and mobile utility platform.

## Features

### 1. 3D / 2D Avatar Assistant (Home)
- **Interactive Avatar**: Dr. Munna virtual assistant with real bundled avatar imagery.
- **Costume Selector**:
  - Doctor's Coat (ডক্টরস অ্যাপ্রন — Clinical advice)
  - Traditional Sari (ঐতিহ্যবাহী শাড়ি — Family & wellness)
  - Salwar Three-piece (মার্জিত থ্রি-পিস — Adolescent health)
  - Casual Modern (ক্যাজুয়াল — Fitness & nutrition)
  - Professional Blazer (প্রফেশনাল — Executive & studio)
- **Bengali Text-to-Speech (TTS)**: Direct spoken Bengali greetings and advice.
- **Daily Health Tips**: Refreshable health advice with audio playback and category tags.
- **Quick Action Grid**: One-tap access to primary clinical and utility tools.

### 2. AI Health Chat (এআই স্বাস্থ্য চ্যাট)
- **Bilingual Conversational Doctor**: Discusses symptoms, general health, lifestyle, and disease prevention.
- **Speech Recognition (Voice Mic)**: Dictate health queries hands-free in Bengali.
- **Audio Output**: Listen to any response spoken aloud in natural Bengali.
- **Medical Disclaimer**: Clear educational guidance advising when to seek in-person clinical care or call emergency helplines.
- **Quick Suggestion Chips**: Common questions regarding fever, headaches, high blood pressure, and hydration.

### 3. Learn & Anatomy (অ্যানাটমি ও স্বাস্থ্য শিক্ষা)
- **Interactive Anatomy Visualizer**:
  - Real Human Body Overview
  - Internal Organs System (`anatomy_organs` visualizer with interactive hotspots for Brain, Heart, Lungs, and Digestive tract)
  - Skeletal & Muscular System (206 bones, bone marrow, and posture)
- **Health Library Articles**:
  - Anatomy & Organ Systems
  - Reproductive Health & Biological Processes
  - Menstrual Health Management
  - Consent & Safe Relationships
  - Contraception & STI Prevention
  - Physical Hygiene & Red Flag Symptoms

### 4. Creative AI Studio (ক্রিয়েটিভ স্টুডিও)
- **Movie & Video Maker**:
  - Meena Cartoon Movie (মিনা ও মিঠু টিয়া পাখি)
  - Stage Concert Songs (মঞ্চে সরাসরি গান)
  - Poetry Recital (কাব্যমঞ্চে আবৃত্তি)
  - Movie Clips (সিনেমা নাটক দৃশ্য)
  - 16:9 Landscape & 9:16 Shorts/Reel Aspect Ratio selection
- **Text Lyrics & Script Generator**: Write Bengali song lyrics, poetry, dialogues, and scripts with copy, share, and spoken recital.
- **AI Image Studio**: Generate medical diagrams and view bundled anatomical art.

### 5. Mobile Utilities & Tools (মোবাইল কন্ট্রোল)
- **Emergency Helplines**: One-tap dialing for Bangladesh National Emergency (999), Shastho Batayon (16263), Women & Children Helpline (109), and 333.
- **BD Phone Number & Carrier Validator**: Validates 11-digit BD mobile numbers (GP, Robi, Banglalink, Teletalk) with BTRC telecom compliance.
- **BMI Calculator**: Body Mass Index calculator with health category classifications and lifestyle recommendations.
- **Water Tracker**: Daily 8-glass hydration target tracker with progress bar and notifications.
- **Medication / Pill Reminder**: Schedule and manage prescription doses with active toggles.
- **Developer Code Templates**: Kotlin, Python, and JavaScript modules for Dr. Munna integrations.
- **Settings**: Google Gemini API key configuration, custom backend URL, voice speech rate/pitch sliders, and audio toggles.

## Tech Stack
- **Framework**: React 19, TypeScript, Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS (Dark theme matching `#0b1020` palette), Lucide Icons
- **Speech**: Web Speech API (`SpeechSynthesis` & `webkitSpeechRecognition`)
- **Effects**: Canvas Confetti
